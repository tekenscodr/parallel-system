# National position voting rules

Source: user instructions for the 2026 election. These are application rules supplied by the user, not a certification of party electoral regulations.

## Electorates

| Position | Eligible electorate |
| --- | --- |
| National Chairperson | All constituency, regional and national executives, plus TESCON Presidents. |
| Vice Chairperson | Same electorate as National Chairperson. |
| General Secretary | Same electorate as National Chairperson. |
| Treasurer | Same electorate as National Chairperson. |
| Communication | Same electorate as National Chairperson. |
| Organiser | Same electorate as National Chairperson. |
| Youth Organiser | Constituency, regional and national executives below 40, plus all TESCON executives excluding patrons regardless of age (confirmed by the user). |
| Women Organiser | All female constituency, regional and national executives, plus all TESCON WOCOM. |
| Nasara Organiser | Nasara executives at constituency, regional and national levels, including TESCON Nasara executives. |

## Counting and data rules

- Use authoritative executive records in ec-data, not geography inferred from campaign contact polling-station assignments.
- Exclude electoral-area and polling-station executives from these national electorates. External branches are not included unless separately authorized.
- Use 2026 minus DOB year for age, as requested in this conversation. Do not modify DOB to make a person eligible. Missing or conflicting age information must be reported for review.
- Determine female eligibility from recorded gender. Do not infer gender from names or photographs. TESCON WOCOM qualify by their recorded office.
- Include substantive and deputy Nasara offices. Exclude TESCON patrons wherever TESCON eligibility applies.
- Count a person once per contest; a person may qualify for multiple different contests. Do not add contest totals together as a unique-person total.
- Use voter ID where available to reconcile identities; flag conflicts and missing identifiers. Do not merge different people solely because they share a name or phone number.
- Report regional and constituency metrics for each contest, with National and unresolved jurisdictions separately identified. Never assign missing geography to a real constituency by default.
- Retain detail supporting each count, eligibility reasons, and records requiring review. Vacant positions are not voters.
- These reports do not certify consent, voting registration, or final electoral approval.

## Deliverables

System breakdowns and an Excel workbook covering the nine positions, regional metrics, constituency metrics, supporting electorate detail and review exceptions.
